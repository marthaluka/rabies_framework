# A framework for evaluating rabies control and preventin strategies

Working repo. 


![](https://github.com/marthaluka/rabies_framework/blob/main/output/framework.png)


Figure 1. Conceptual framework for the linked transmission and probabilistic decision model.
A) Human rabies outcomes are modelled from dog-rabies incidence through bite exposure, healthcare seeking, 
PEP initiation and completion, and subsequent risk of death. Rabid and non-rabid dog bites are represented 
separately, with intervention costs accumulated along the pathway. B) Integrated Bite Case Management (IBCM) 
links bite-patient presentation to risk assessments and results in the probabilistic investigation and 
testing of potentially rabid dogs.
